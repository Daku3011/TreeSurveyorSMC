📌 How Carbon Stock is Calculated

Using tropical tree allometric equation:
AGB = 0.0509 × DBH² × H
Carbon = AGB × 0.5 (50% biomass is carbon)

⚠️ Health Score Formula

Excellent=4pts, Good=3pts, Fair=2pts, Poor=1pt
Score = (Weighted Sum) / (Total × 4) × 100%
